# MOC Antagonists

Roster of opponents and culprits, with **function**, **visibility**, and build
**status**. Authoring guidance lives in
[[99_System/Templates/Template_Antagonist|Template_Antagonist]]; tone rules in
`docs/ATMOSPHERE_BIBLE.md`.

Design principle: Case 01 has **no single villain**. It is a layered cover-up —
a respectable culprit, hired specialists, false trails, and a mind that stays
off-screen. Protect that structure: never collapse the layers into one boss.

## Case 01 — Bankhaus J.A. Krebs

- **Heinrich Galdermann** — [[30_World_Intel/Characters/char_bank_manager|char_bank_manager]]
  - Function: cover / orchestrator of the public robbery narrative.
  - Visibility: on-screen (public face of the cover-up).
  - Confrontation: lawful (confession) or negotiated (partial truth) at the warehouse.
  - Status: runtime.
- **Oberleutnant Albrecht Stoll, "The Sapper"** — [[30_World_Intel/Characters/char_military_engineer|char_military_engineer]]
  - Function: executor / specialist (thermite breach, drove the postal car).
  - Visibility: flashback-first (hands/shadow before face), then warehouse reveal.
  - Confrontation: violent stand or negotiated step-aside (trades the Architect's shape).
  - Status: design-only (promotion candidate).
- **The Architect** — (no dossier; off-screen by design)
  - Function: hidden planner / target selection; Stoll's unnamed former commander.
  - Visibility: named-but-absent — stays hidden past Case 01 into the University thread.
  - Confrontation: none in Case 01 (a hook, not a fight).
  - Status: design lore only.
- **Emil Roth** — [[40_GameViewer/Case01/_Characters/char_case01_restorer_roth|char_case01_restorer_roth]]
  - Function: false-trail / enabler (leaked the Razlom box measurements).
  - Visibility: on-screen (grimoire false trail).
  - Status: runtime.
- **Krebs Mugger** — [[40_GameViewer/Case01/_Characters/char_case01_krebs_mugger|char_case01_krebs_mugger]]
  - Function: street pressure / enforcer (Free Yards contract). Not part of the vault breach.
  - Visibility: on-screen (witch alley line).
  - Status: runtime.

### Pressure figures (false trails, not core culprits)
