# Victoria Sterling

![Assistant](apps/web/public/images/characters/assistant.png)

> **Role**: Assistant / Forensic Scientist
> **Archetype**: The Pioneer
> **Voice**: `Senses`

## Profile

Severe, practical dress (_Reformkleid_), ink-stained fingers, smells faintly of
chemicals. Carries a heavy leather satchel like a weapon. Rigid, arms-crossed
posture.

Victoria Adlersheim Sterling is the Oberbuergermeister's daughter and uses
**Sterling** as her married name after her husband's death. The Rathaus can open
doors for her, but the Polizeidirektion refuses to recognize her as an official
criminologist; Case 01 makes her authority useful, contested, and never
ornamental.

Her birth family, the [[30_World_Intel/Families/family_adlersheim_sterling|Adlersheims]],
love through control. Her father opens doors only in forms the city can deny,
her mother taught composure as a survival discipline, and her siblings learned
to read family reputation as a civic instrument. Victoria's rebellion is not
against being unloved; it is against being made safe at the cost of the truth.

**hates::** [[Ernst_Schiller]] (Scientific Rivalry)
**hates::** [[Heinrich_Galdermann]] (Suspects him)
**works_for::** [[Freiburg_University]]

## Secrets

**Explicit Motive**: Prove her scientific competence.
**Hidden Motive**: Her husband was murdered after discovering a new method of
synthesizing Saccharin. She is hunting his killers, but Case 01 only lets that
old wound cast a shadow.

**Internal Conflict**: **Logic vs. Rage**.

## Family

- **Otto Adlersheim** - father, Oberbuergermeister; protective leverage and
  political containment in the same hand.
- **Margarethe Adlersheim** - mother; keeper of presentation and the source of
  Victoria's polished social restraint.
- **Konstantin Adlersheim** - older brother; respectable civic heir who respects
  Victoria's mind and distrusts her public methods.
- **Anneliese Adlersheim** - younger sister; salon and poetry contact whose
  listening can seed the Victoria poetry thread without making her a runtime NPC.
- **Dr. Julian Sterling** - late husband; chemist whose murder remains a
  long-form shadow, not a Case 01 solution.
- **Edmund Sterling** - father-in-law; patent-law/industry contact who keeps
  part of Julian's papers outside Rathaus custody.
