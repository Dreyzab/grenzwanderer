# Victoria Sterling

## Dossier

- **Role**: Mayor's widowed daughter, unofficial scientific consultant, possible assistant to the detective, and volatile personal stakes.
- **Age / Appearance**: 23, severe lab posture, restless focus.
- **Archetype**: The Vengeful Analyst
- **Origin**: Freiburg university chemistry circles; Victoria Adlersheim by birth, daughter of the Oberbuergermeister. Her old friendship with Lotte Weber predates the case and gives her one non-institutional anchor in the city.
- **Public Name**: Frau Sterling, the married name she kept after her husband's death.
- **Case01 Status**: Private scientific consultant attached under Matthias Adler's responsibility after the Polizeidirektor refuses to recognize a woman criminologist.
- **Compatibility Note**: `assistant` is a legacy role key, not a permanent identity. Felix can occupy an early assistant/logistics role; Victoria occupies the scientific consultant role from Rathaus/Bank onward.

## Secrets

- **Surface**: Supports forensic branch of case
- **Hidden**: Keeps unsanctioned notes on her husband's prior death; the official poisoning explanation does not fit what she knows of his chemistry work.
- **Core**: Volition reveals vigilante impulse
- **Case01 Rule**: The bank residue can echo the old death, but Case 01 must not solve it outright. This is Victoria's character quest / shadow case (`qst_victoria_shadow_case`), not a second main investigation.

## Family

Family bible: [[30_World_Intel/Families/family_adlersheim_sterling|Adlersheim-Sterling Family]].

- **Otto Adlersheim** - father and Oberbuergermeister; his help is real, but every writ is shaped to be deniable.
- **Margarethe Adlersheim** - mother; trained Victoria's public composure and treats reputation as care.
- **Konstantin Adlersheim** - older brother; civic heir, wary of Victoria's unsanctioned methods.
- **Anneliese Adlersheim** - younger sister; salon/poetry listener and soft key for `qst_victoria_poetry`, not a first-pass runtime NPC.
- **Dr. Julian Sterling** - late husband; Freiburg University chemist and lecturer whose alleged poisoning became the wound that pulled Victoria toward forensic criminology. His murder is shadow context only in Case 01.
- **Edmund Sterling** - father-in-law; keeper of some Sterling papers outside both Rathaus and university control.
