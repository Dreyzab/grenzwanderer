## Dossier

- **Role**: The technical hand of the Bankhaus Krebs job — the officer who cut the breach charge, melted the private deposit box, and walked the closed postal car out of the square in broad daylight. The crime's *method*, not its *mind*.
- **Rank / Posting**: **Oberleutnant der Pioniere**, attached to the Freiburg garrison engineer detachment (bridging, demolition, field fortification). Still serving — not a discharged veteran.
- **Age / Appearance**: 40s. Broad-shouldered, parade-straight posture even off duty. Calloused hands marked with old chemical burns; one knuckle scar he rubs when he calculates.
- **Archetype**: The Clockwork Breacher — a disciplined man who treats a robbery as an engineering problem and sleeps fine because the math closed.
- **Origin**: Career sapper raised in the Pionier tradition; competent, passed over for promotion, quietly in debt. The garrison gave him explosives doctrine; the city gave him reasons to sell it.

## Era Note

Under Kaiser-era garrison practice an engineer officer signs for demolition stores, requisition forms, and unit dispatch paper. Stoll's binding authority over **dispatch letterhead and movement orders** is what makes the forged postal route plausible — the false "military dispatch" false trail leads back through a real signature he had every right to make. His crime is institutional access misused, not break-in from outside.

## Function in Crime

- **Function**: Executor / specialist (point breach), plus the body that drove the closed postal car.
- **Not**: Not the planner of the cover-up (that is Galdermann) and not the hidden mind behind the target selection (the "Architect," who stays off-screen past Case 01).
- **Contribution to the theatre**: precision. Workers supplied noise, the post route supplied movement, Roth supplied the box's measurements — Stoll supplied the clean, surgical melt that makes the raid read as "expensive theatre for one private box."

## Motivation & Justification

- **Lever**: Standing gambling/garrison-mess debts held, ultimately, by a House-of-Pledges line that Galdermann can call in. He was *bought with his own ledger*.
- **Self-justification**: Professional pride. He frames it as a demolition contract, not theft — "I burned one box and left the building standing. A thief would have wrecked the room." The cleanliness is how he keeps his conscience.
- **Architect hook**: He takes target data and timing from a former commanding officer he still defers to by reflex. He never says the name. The deference itself is a clue that someone above him exists.
- **Moral weight (earned darkness)**: A serving officer renting out imperial demolition doctrine to cover a banker's embezzlement. The rot is that the uniform made the crime *possible and respectable*, not that he is a monster.

## Secrets

- **Surface**: A garrison engineer officer, stretched thin by duty rotations and quietly behind on his debts.
- **Hidden**: Used off-shift daytime hours to drive the disguised closed postal car and execute the breach unnoticed, then signed the forged dispatch that gave the route its cover.
- **Core**: Sourced magnesium and iron oxide through the Apothecary to mix the thermite himself, and takes his target list from an unnamed former commander — the only thread that points past Galdermann toward the Architect. Reachable only by Authority/Deception pressure that makes him slip into reporting posture.

## Evolution

- **Stage 1** (start): A phantom. Known only by thermite slag, a wrong wheel mark, and a postal car that sat badly in the square.
- **Stage 2** (after leads unlock): Resolved into a person — Apothecary chain (reagents) + post-route anomaly (forged dispatch) + convergence beat "Not A Thief, An Engineer" name the *role* before the *face*.
- **Stage 3** (finale): Appears at the warehouse as the technical guard beside Galdermann. Offers a fork — a violent stand for the man who paid him, or a negotiated step-aside that trades his testimony (and the shape of the Architect) for a quiet exit.

## Exposure Path

- **Residue → Perception**: vault thermite slag + burn-darkened cuffs → "a trained demolition hand."
- **Dispatch → Logic / Authority**: forged military dispatch letterhead → an officer with signing authority, not an outsider.
- **Reagents → Senses / Deception**: Apothecary's private buyer key → the specific man who mixed the thermite.
- **Motive → Empathy / Authority**: debt lever + reporting posture → why he did it, and the unnamed commander above him.
