# Adlersheim-Sterling Family

## Family Function

The Adlersheim-Sterling family is Victoria's lore pressure layer, not a new
Case 01 runtime branch. Its core emotional grammar is **love as control**:
Victoria is protected, funded, and granted access by people who also try to
make her safe, deniable, and socially legible.

Case 01 should surface the family through names, letters, reputation, political
pressure, and the old wound of Julian Sterling's death. It must not solve the
Sterling murder thread.

## Core Members

### [[30_World_Intel/Characters/char_assistant|Victoria Adlersheim Sterling]]

- **Public name**: Frau Sterling.
- **Birth family**: Adlersheim.
- **Role**: Widowed chemist-criminologist, unofficial scientific consultant.
- **Family wound**: She is loved by a civic machine that keeps converting her
  competence into a liability to be managed.

### [[30_World_Intel/Characters/char_mayor|Otto Adlersheim]]

- **Role**: Oberbuergermeister and public face of civic order.
- **Family function**: Opens doors for Victoria only in forms the Rathaus can
  deny later.
- **Emotional rule**: He fears for Victoria before he fears her, but every act
  of protection becomes a restriction.

### Margarethe Adlersheim

- **Role**: Keeper of family presentation, invitations, and scandal boundaries.
- **Family function**: Taught Victoria composure, dress, timing, and the art of
  making anger invisible.
- **Emotional rule**: She calls control "care" and means it.

### Konstantin Adlersheim

- **Role**: Older brother and future civic administrator.
- **Family function**: The acceptable heir to Otto's public order.
- **Emotional rule**: Respects Victoria's intelligence but believes her methods
  endanger the family name.

### Anneliese Adlersheim

- **Role**: Younger sister with salon and poetry access.
- **Family function**: Soft cultural key for `qst_victoria_poetry`.
- **Emotional rule**: Hears what the family dismisses as empty talk, including
  coded verses, dedications, and social route markers.

### Dr. Julian Sterling

- **Role**: Victoria's late husband, chemist.
- **Family function**: The unresolved Sterling wound.
- **Case 01 rule**: Chemical residue may rhyme with his death, but his murder
  remains shadow context.

### Edmund Sterling

- **Role**: Julian's father; patent lawyer or industrial intermediary.
- **Family function**: Holds part of Julian's papers and distrusts both the
  Rathaus and university channels.
- **Emotional rule**: Treats Victoria as the only person with the right grief,
  but not always the right judgment.

## Runtime Boundary

- Do not add runtime NPC identities, sprites, or services for Margarethe,
  Konstantin, Anneliese, Julian, or Edmund in the first pass.
- Otto may continue using the existing mayor runtime identity.
- Victoria remains `victoria_sterling`; `assistant` remains compatibility-only.
