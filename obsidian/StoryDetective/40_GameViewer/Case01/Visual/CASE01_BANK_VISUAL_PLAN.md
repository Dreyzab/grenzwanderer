# Case 01 Bank Investigation Visual Plan

## Core Rule

Everything staged by people should look composed, procedural, and theatrical.
Everything left by the crime should look physical, ugly, and specific. The bank
is trying to perform dignity; the evidence refuses to help.

All generated VN background plates must follow the project art contract:
Sargent-style oil painting, broad expressive brushstrokes, visible canvas
texture, 1905 Kaiser-era Germany, 16:9, and sprite-safe composition. Background
plates should not contain visible people; character/witness presence is handled
by sprites, text, or later hermetic cutscenes.

## Continuity Motifs

- Black-yellow postal twine: postal car, gas canister, route slip, later
  evidence table.
- Brass and marble: institutional prestige made faintly unclean by chemical
  residue.
- Almost invisible gas: never an opaque green cloud; show it through sunbeams,
  yellowed wet cloths, dulled brass, gray skin, and a wilting lobby plant.
- Official paper too early: printed robbery reports, adjusted document stacks,
  blotter marks, and hidden ledger edges.

## Scene 1: Bank Arrival

Location: Bankhaus J.A. Krebs, Freiburg Muensterplatz.

Exterior direction:

- High-contrast daylight on a prestigious baroque/civic facade.
- A closed postal car is parked awkwardly near the entrance.
- The rear door is not fully latched.
- A torn route slip or loose manifest sits near one wheel.
- One wheel or mud mark should feel wrong for the street.

Lobby direction:

- Marble and brass remain opulent, but the room has lost dignity.
- Narrative layer: clerks slump on mahogany benches with wet cloths, not heroic wounds.
- Background plate: show the benches, abandoned wet cloths, yellowed stains, and
  disturbed posture of the room, but no visible clerks.
- Gas is nearly invisible: visible only in shafts of light and stained cloths.
- The room should feel quiet in a rehearsed way, as if everyone has been told
  which version of panic is acceptable.

Key contrast: expensive architecture, undignified staff.

## Scene 2: Director Galdermann's Office

Location: upper floor office overlooking the square.

Visual direction:

- Heavy oak desk, velvet curtains, filtered daylight, flickering gas lamps.
- Galdermann is polished but sweat-beaded.
- He keeps adjusting the official robbery report so it covers ledger material.
- Use smelling salts on the desk, not a discarded gas mask.
- Add one too-early detail: printed report pages, a damp palm mark on blotter
  paper, or a burned corner of a postal route slip in the ashtray.
- The window may be slightly open, quietly contradicting his claim that the gas
  reached everywhere.

Key prop: half-empty smelling salts beside over-prepared official paperwork.

Avoid: putting a gas mask on his desk. That makes the lie too blunt too early.

## Scene 3: Vault

Location: deep basement vault.

Composition:

- First read: the main vault is open and nearly empty.
- Second read: one private deposit box is violently melted.
- The rest of the steel should look clean enough to make the one ruined box
  feel like a wound.

Clues:

- Thermite slag: dark metallic drips on floor and lock throat.
- Gas canister: modified fumigation canister abandoned near a service grate.
- Postal twine: roughly tied around the canister handle.
- Razlom box: empty private box with scorched velvet lining.

Key visual sentence: money was already gone; the raid was expensive theatre for
one private box.

## Character Visuals

### Military Engineer, "The Sapper"

- Broad-shouldered silhouette.
- Modified postman's coat that fits badly over a grease-stained military tunic.
- Heavy chemical-resistant gloves, not postal gloves.
- Army seam or unit stitching visible under one sleeve.
- Metallic dust on bag edges or cuffs.
- Prefer hands, shoulders, and shadow in flashbacks before revealing his face.

### Herr Kiliani

- Meticulous, nervous, controlled.
- Perfect handwritten labels and disciplined shelves.
- Jars imply magnesium, sulfur, iron oxide, and solvents.
- One shelf or counter patch has been wiped too clean.
- Clean hands, but gray powder under one nail or around a cuff.
- He should read as someone who supplied material under a false story, then
  understood too late what it enabled.

## Generation Notes

- Freiburg, not Fribourg.
- Use the `.agent/assets/base_style.md` 7-slot prompt contract.
- Use S1: `Oil painting, broad expressive brushstrokes, visible canvas texture`.
- Use S2: `1905 Kaiser-era Germany, historical veracity`.
- Use S7 global avoid: plastic textures, flat digital art, CGI rendering, 3D
  smooth faces, modern photography, neon colors, generic 21st-century fashion.
- VN background plates: no visible people, no clerks, no police silhouettes.
- No opaque green gas cloud.
- No crowded hero-action staging for investigation backgrounds.
- Postal evidence should be real, not just a red herring.
- The gas mask is a later stash/reveal prop, not Galdermann's opening-office
  tell.
