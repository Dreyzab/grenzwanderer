# Victoria Sterling

**Role**: Widowed unofficial scientific consultant / companion investigator
**Public Status**: Frau Sterling, widow, born Victoria Adlersheim, daughter of the Oberbuergermeister
**Runtime Character ID**: `victoria_sterling`
**Compatibility Note**: `assistant` remains a legacy role key for older bridge
content and should not be treated as one fixed person.

## Profile

- University-trained forensic analyst with chemistry expertise.
- Refused official recognition by the Polizeidirektion because she is a woman.
- Attached to Matthias Adler privately through the Rathaus writ.
- Old friend of Lotte Weber; their trust predates the case and gives Victoria a personal channel outside Rathaus procedure.
- Her late husband, Dr. Julian Sterling, was a Freiburg University chemist and lecturer. The official poisoning explanation never fit his competence, which is why grief became forensic obsession rather than only mourning.
- Uses the Bankhaus Krebs case to pursue truth while avoiding premature answers
  about her husband's death.
- Treat this as a character quest / shadow case: Bankhaus Krebs may echo the
  Sterling death through chemical residue, university silences, and official
  lies, but Case 01 must not solve Julian Sterling's death outright.
- Her Adlersheim family loves through control: Otto opens deniable doors,
  Margarethe enforces social composure, Konstantin protects the family name, and
  Anneliese can seed the poetry thread as a cultural contact without becoming a
  first-pass runtime NPC.

## Case 01 Function

- Interprets gas, residue, postal twine, and route timing.
- Converts the mayor's political favor into limited archive and bank access.
- Rewards professional respect; cools toward patronizing treatment.
- Keeps slow-burn undertones optional and grief-aware.
- Keeps the Sterling murder as shadow context only; Case 01 may echo it through

- Carries `qst_victoria_shadow_case` as a trust-and-restraint arc rather than a
