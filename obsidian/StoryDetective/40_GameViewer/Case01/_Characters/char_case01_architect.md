# The Architect

**Runtime id**: `npc_architect` (content id only; do not render as an NPC)
**Role**: Off-stage planner
**Archetype**: The Absent Architect

## Profile

- No face, no dossier portrait, no direct lines in Case01 by design.
- Former commander of Stoll. His orders still live in Stoll's body like an old command shouted once too often.
- Felt only by the shape of the hole he leaves: the target is too precise, Razlom is too specific, and the false trails are too competent for a thief.
- Smells of paperwork and powder: bureaucracy with demolition memory.

## Case01 Function

- Cannot be caught in Case01.
- Must be outlined, not revealed.
- The player should leave with a silhouette and a thread into the university line.

## Guardrails

- Do not give him a portrait.
- Do not give him a direct conversation.
- Do not let Galdermann or Stoll name him cleanly.
- Do not make him a generic masked villain. His absence is the weapon.
