# Heinrich Galdermann

![Portrait](/images/characters/heinrich_galdermann/heinrich_galdermann.webp)

**Runtime id**: `npc_heinrich_galdermann`
**Role**: Prokurist, Bankhaus J.A. Krebs
**Archetype**: Respectable Culprit
**Roster tier**: major

## Profile

- Broad as an iron safe, dressed in a waistcoat worth more than a clerk's yearly pay.
- Smiles like a notarized signature: polite, binding, and designed to make refusal feel improper.
- Sweat gathers at the roots of his perfect side part. His handkerchief is not a prop but a repeated motion.
- On his desk: smelling salts, a grossbuch, and the robbery report he keeps sliding over the ledger like a child hiding a broken cup under a napkin.
- He did not kill, burn, or steal by hand. He signed. The crime passed through a pen, not a crowbar.

## Investigation Function

- Public face of the Case01 closure. He can be exposed and arrested; this gives the city a culprit.
- He is not the top of the chain. Under pressure he either breaks into confession or bargains with half the truth, but he will not name the person above him.
- The horror of him is institutional: respectability makes the crime easier to process, file, and deny.

## Visual Anchors

- Wide, expensive, over-groomed banker body.
- Damp hairline and handkerchief gesture.
- Report covering ledger; smelling salts nearby.
- Avoid: street thief, cartoon villain, thin nervous clerk, modern finance executive.
