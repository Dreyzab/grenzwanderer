# Karl

![Portrait](/images/characters/karl_servant/karl_servant.webp)

**Runtime id**: `npc_karl_servant`
**Role**: Grand Estate servant — pantry, service corridors, supply runs
**Affiliation**: House of Pledges (estate household staff)
**Roster tier**: functional

## Profile

- First crossed at HBF, when a luggage clasp cuts his palm and Eleonora chooses
  whether to drink the blood or bandage it.
- At Grand Estate he holds the service corridors — the smuggling-grade
  passage between cellar, pantry, and outer yard that the Baroness's partners
  used to route the ledger out of the house.
- Memory and willingness are conditional on how Eleonora treated him: a
  bandaged hand earns a name, a fed-on body earns a panicked silence the
  estate later reads as ghost-noise.

## Service

- `svc_karl_service_corridors` — quiet access to the estate's service
  passages and pantry routes, unlocked when Karl trusts Eleonora.
