# Anton Weber

![Portrait](/images/characters/anton_weber/anton_weber.webp)

**Runtime id**: `npc_anton_weber`
**Role**: Reichspost route clerk
**Affiliation**: Freiburg HBF / Reichspost
**Roster tier**: functional

## Profile

- Postman whose real route was bent by a forged military order.
- Black-yellow twine is his professional handwriting; the job turned it into evidence against everyone.
- Nervous, meticulous, proud of route discipline, and ashamed that official grammar made refusal feel like treason.

## Investigation Function

- Supplies movement, not motive.
- His route makes the postal-car false trail believable and physically possible.
- Press him too brutally and you punish the wrong layer while the forged order's author stays clean.
