# Oberleutnant Albrecht Stoll

![Portrait](/images/characters/albrecht_stoll/albrecht_stoll.webp)

**Runtime id**: `npc_albrecht_stoll`
**Role**: Serving pioneer officer / The Sapper
**Archetype**: The Sapper
**Roster tier**: major

## Profile

- Shoulders like a bridge he is used to charging at the supports.
- The postal coat sits wrong on him. A tunic seam shows under one sleeve; the uniform refuses to pretend.
- He keeps chemical gloves on even in conversation. Old thermite soot marks the cuffs.
- He rubs a scarred knuckle when calculating in his head.
- He does not deny thermite. He denies being a thief: he burned one box and left the building standing.

## Investigation Function

- The method, not the mind. Stoll supplies precision: thermite, timing, forged military authority, and the clean cut.
- Workers supplied noise; the postal route supplied movement; Roth supplied target data; Stoll supplied the surgical breach.
- His discipline is the danger. He sleeps because the math balanced.
- He obeys an unnamed former commander almost bodily. That deference is the Architect clue; the name is never given in Case01.

## Visual Anchors

- Broad, formal, military posture under a bad postal disguise.
- Chemical gloves, burn-dark cuffs, old knuckle scar.
- Uniform seam visible beneath civilian/postal coat.
- Avoid: decorated parade hero, wild pyromaniac, street robber, fantasy sapper.
