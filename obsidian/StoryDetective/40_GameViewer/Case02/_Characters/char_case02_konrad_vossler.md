# Konrad Vossler

![Portrait](/images/characters/konrad_vossler/konrad_vossler.webp)

**Runtime id**: `npc_konrad_vossler`
**Role**: Chemistry teacher / former pioneer
**Case**: Dead Registry / Case02
**Roster tier**: functional

## Profile

- Former pioneer from the same demolition school as Stoll.
- Sulfur on the cuffs, a teacher's hands, and a voice that cuts off too sharply.
- Not an accomplice. A mirror.
- He is what Stoll could have become if he had once said no and survived the cost of refusal.

## Guardrail

Do not merge Vossler with the Sapper arc. He exists to show that training creates capacity, not destiny.
